8D problem solving, inside Jira

Eight-D walks a quality team through an 8D report on the Jira work item where the problem already lives. A Rovo coach drafts; a person approves every step.

What it does

  • 8D panel on each work item. D1 team to D8 close, with a progress bar, text fields and an Approve button per step.
  • 8D Overview page. Every open 8D you are allowed to see, with current step, containment clock and days open.
  • 8D Coach (Rovo agent). Drafts the D2 problem statement in 5W2H form, scaffolds a 5-Why, finds similar past cases and previews corrective actions. It drafts only; it never approves.
  • Read-only tools for your AI client. The same read-only tools are available through Atlassian's MCP server, once your admin turns them on.

Built to keep a person in charge

  • Every D-step is approved by a signed-in person; the approver and date are stored.
  • Work items with the label safety switch the automation off and point to your quality manager.
  • Facts that are not on the work item stay "unknown"; nothing is invented. It never scores or ranks people.

Your data stays in your Atlassian site

Eight-D runs on Atlassian Forge. It keeps its records in Forge storage inside your own Atlassian site, calls no outside servers and has no tracking. See Privacy and Security.
