Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Eight-D app for Jira ("Eight-D"), made by THATCHED Ltd ("we"). In short: Eight-D keeps your 8D records inside your own Atlassian site, sends nothing to us or anyone else, and has no tracking.

What data Eight-D handles

  • 8D records. The text you type into the 8D steps (team, problem, containment, root cause, actions, close), each step's approval state, the approver's Atlassian account ID and the approval date. One record per Jira work item.
  • Work items you can already see. Eight-D reads Jira work items and, if you use them, Confluence pages through Atlassian's APIs as the signed-in user, so it shows only what Jira and Confluence already let that person see.
  • Teamwork Graph objects (optional). If the connector is on, Eight-D writes part, machine and supplier-lot objects to Atlassian's Teamwork Graph in your site.

Where it is stored

All of this is stored in Atlassian Forge hosted storage and Atlassian products in your Atlassian site. Atlassian hosts it under its own terms and data-residency settings. Eight-D declares no outside network access: no data leaves Atlassian's platform, and THATCHED has no copy of it and no way to read it.

What we do not do

  • No third parties, no sub-processors of our own, no outside servers.
  • No analytics, cookies, tracking or advertising inside the app.
  • No selling or sharing of data.
  • The app does not write your 8D content to its logs.

AI features

The 8D Coach is a Rovo agent. Its drafts are produced by Atlassian Rovo inside your Atlassian site, under Atlassian's own terms for Rovo. Eight-D does not send your data to any other AI service.

How long it is kept

8D records are kept until you delete them or uninstall the app. After uninstall, Atlassian removes the app's stored data under Forge's data-retention rules.

Roles under GDPR

Your organisation is the controller of the content in your work items and 8D reports. For that content, THATCHED acts only as a processor: the app processes it on your instructions inside Atlassian's platform, and we do not access it.

Your rights and requests

Because the data sits in your Atlassian site, your site admins can view, export or delete it there. For any privacy question or request, write to business@thatched.ai.
